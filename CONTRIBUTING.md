# Contributing

Contributions should improve the clarity or verification of this public case
study without reconstructing a private system.

## Publication boundary

- Keep identities, organizations, records, modules, and workflows fictional.
- Use reserved example domains in every sample.
- Do not add private code, prose, tests, schemas, migrations, screenshots,
  routes, topology, credentials, recovery details, or interface assets.
- Do not add quantitative outcomes without an approved claim record containing
  a baseline, method, sample, limitation, and disclosure authority.
- Describe external providers by capability and preserve their authority for
  their own records.
- Keep equivalent prose adjacent to every diagram.

Run the local verification before proposing a change:

```bash
bash tests/verify.sh
```

Documentation contributions are accepted under CC BY 4.0. Contributions to
`samples/`, `tests/`, or `.github/workflows/` are accepted under MIT. By
contributing, you confirm that you may license the submitted material on those
terms.
